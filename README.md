## Hi, I'm Ibrahim 👋

**AI Product Engineer: I take agentic AI and LLM features from spec to production, along with the full-stack SaaS around them.**

5+ years shipping production systems for US insurance and healthcare platforms, plus generative-AI products built end to end. I currently manage a 12-person engineering team, and have led and worked across cross-functional teams with US product and engineering stakeholders throughout my career.

---

### What I build

- **Agentic AI:** AI agents and agentic workflows with tool calling, guardrails and human-in-the-loop steps
- **LLM applications:** OpenAI and Claude integrations, Model Context Protocol (MCP), RAG and knowledge-base assistants, prompt design
- **AI automation:** n8n workflows with AI steps (classify, extract, draft) wired into CRMs, email, spreadsheets, databases and webhooks
- **Generative media:** video, voice and music models (Veo 3, Kling, Sora, ElevenLabs, Suno) orchestrated in one product workflow
- **Production SaaS:** TypeScript, Next.js, Node.js, Supabase, Stripe billing, AWS Lambda, and event-driven integrations
